## §1. Setup

The 3-face Hilbert filter `(♦_ab) ∧ (♦_bc) ∧ (♦_ac)` at `m ≤ 1000` admits **52 non-degenerate** primitive Saunderson fibers (`three_face_scale_1000.md`). Every survivor obeys: `m ≡ 1 (mod 4)`, `n` even, `sf(P)`, `sf(Q)` each a product of two primes `≡ 1 (mod 8)`, all four primes in pairwise mutual non-residue. Saunderson Trichotomy on the non-branch locus of `H_q: Z² = (Y²−d_ab²)(Y²−a²)(Y²−b²)` (with `a = m²−n²`, `b = 2mn`, `d_ab = m²+n²`):

- **Case (i)**: all three `αᵢ = Y²−eᵢ²` individually square ⇒ PCP brick at fiber, equivalently Saunderson `c` rational with `c²+a²`, `c²+b²`, `c²+d_ab²` all squares. Search: rational `c = a(q²−p²)/(2pq)` indexed by coprime `(p, q)`. Trivial `c = 0` at `p = 0` only.
- **Case (ii)**: exactly one `αᵢ` square ⇒ Heron face conic `V_{P,±Q}`. For our 52, all three sign-conics are LOCALLY SOLVABLE — that is the survivor condition. So Case (ii) does NOT close here at Hilbert level (unlike `(73, 24)` and `(63, 38)`, where ≥ 2 conics fail).
- **Case (iii)**: none individually square ⇒ generic cross-pair 2-Selmer class on `E_Hm`.
